Understanding the "Cathedral Effect"

Mechanism of the Cathedral Effect:

Small Visual Fields (Low Ceilings): When operating in spaces with low ceilings or small visual fields, your thinking tends to become "smaller"—more focused on analytic processing and detailed work. This promotes a more concentrated, granular cognitive modality.
Expansive Visual Fields (High Ceilings): Conversely, working in high-ceiling spaces elicits abstract thoughts and creativity. This environment encourages a broader, more expansive cognitive processing style.
Sensitivity: Even relatively minor differences, such as a two-foot discrepancy in ceiling height, have been shown to elicit these distinct cognitive shifts.
